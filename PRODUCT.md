# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone who looks Ryan up: peers, people he's met, potential employers or collaborators. They want a quick sense of who he is, what he's worked on, and how to reach him. There is no single target audience and no conversion goal.

## Product Purpose

Personal site for Ryan Donohue, a fullstack software developer from Regina, Saskatchewan, at rdonohue.ca. It works as a calling card. Success means a visitor leaves knowing who Ryan is and what he's worked on, and can get in touch if they want to.

## Positioning

It's Ryan's own site, built by him and written the way a developer talks. A LinkedIn profile or a template portfolio can't be either of those.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A single page: who Ryan is, his work, and how to reach him.
- Contact is a plain email link to ryan@rdonohue.ca. Ryan chose this over the old Mailgun form, so the site needs no backend or secrets.
- Hosted on Vercel; rdonohue.ca's DNS points there (as of 2026-10-04 it serves DEPLOYMENT_NOT_FOUND).
- The Work list on the live page doesn't include Sticker Mule yet (see Evidence on Hand).
- A commented-out "What Do I Do?" section holds lorem ipsum. It isn't real content.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ryan Donohue. Domain: rdonohue.ca.
- Ryan's headshot (`public/assets/ryan.jpg`, supplied 2026-10-05) must stay on the site.
- The older roles stay too, reworded in the site's voice.
- Voice: very much a developer's website. Plain, direct and specific. No LinkedIn language: no "passionate", "results-driven", "leveraging", buzzword bios or corporate self-promotion.

## Evidence on Hand

- Photo: `public/assets/ryan.jpg`, supplied by Ryan on 2026-10-05. Older photos and company logos were removed at his request; they're still in git history.
- **Sticker Mule** (current, permanent full-time): Software Engineer since Apr 2021 in Regina, SK; Lead Software Engineer since Aug 2026, remote. Per his LinkedIn, he:
  - led a cross-team cost-cutting initiative on warehouse operations and how customer orders get collected;
  - managed the migration and optimization of the core business algorithm, which made production more efficient and cut costs;
  - led a large infrastructure and code migration for a 400-person department without interrupting operations.

  This comes from Ryan's LinkedIn. Keep the facts but rewrite the wording in the site's voice; never copy the LinkedIn phrasing. Details beyond these lines (what the algorithm does, stack, numbers) haven't been provided.
- Earlier work entries: 54e Dev Studios / OneShot Golf, Outpost Health, Brew Ninja, Vivvo. The current blurbs describe each company, not Ryan's role or what he built. They have no titles or dates.
- Absent: GitHub, social or resume links, testimonials, and titles and dates for the earlier roles. Don't fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. **Reads like a developer wrote it.** Concrete specifics beat adjectives. If a line could appear on anyone's LinkedIn, cut it.
2. **Calling card, not a funnel.** Getting in touch is easy but never pushed.
3. **Real work only.** Every entry and claim traces to something Ryan confirmed.
